# Community expectations

Trivian Institute welcomes careful disagreement across disciplines, cultures, abilities, and experiences. Critique claims and decisions without degrading people. Do not harass, threaten, discriminate, expose personal information, or pressure someone to disclose experiences or participate in a practice. Do not use this space to diagnose contributors or learners.

Ask before quoting another participant's personal account. Do not post private AI transcripts, learner records, or identifiable case material. Use fictional examples when possible. Respect a request to pause or leave a discussion. Accessibility requests need no public medical explanation.

Report concerns privately to learn@trivianinstitute.org with the minimum relevant information. Do not post sensitive evidence publicly. Maintainers will consider context, hear relevant perspectives when feasible, and may request edits, remove content, pause a discussion, or restrict participation. Repeated or severe harm may result in exclusion. Reports involving a maintainer should request review by another Institute representative; this small project cannot promise an independent review body or a specific response time.

Maintainers should explain moderation decisions without revealing private details. A participant may ask for reconsideration through the same contact and provide new information. Confidentiality will be respected as far as practical, but cannot be guaranteed. This repository is an educational collaboration space, not an emergency or clinical support service.
