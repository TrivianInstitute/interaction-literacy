# Contributing to the Trivian Educational Commons

Status: v0.1 · Maturity: formative / educational

Trivian Institute maintains this educational project. Contact: learn@trivianinstitute.org.

## Start with a small contribution

Open an issue describing a confusing passage, an accessibility barrier, a teaching experience, or a proposed resource. Include the file path and the change you recommend. Submit substantial additions through a branch and pull request. A maintainer reviews scope, evidence, accessibility, rights, and fit before a human decides whether to merge. Submission does not establish Institute endorsement or make a framework canonical. Submissions may be edited, returned for revision, or declined with an explanation.

For teaching feedback, describe the setting, approximate group size, activity, difficulty, and proposed improvement. Do not include learner names, identifiable quotations, grades, health information, or private interaction logs. A positive workshop report is useful feedback, not proof that an intervention works.

## Evidence and interpretation

Label empirical claims and link to their source, explaining what it actually supports. Identify limitations and avoid turning a correlation into a causal claim. Distinguish established knowledge, interpretation, Trivian frameworks, philosophical propositions, hypotheses, educational practices, and open questions. A citation alone does not settle a disagreement. Fictional cases must be marked fictional; do not quietly turn someone's experience into a case.

Use plain language and define necessary terms. Offer alternatives to speech, writing, movement, internal bodily attention, and live AI access where relevant. No learner should have to disclose disability, trauma, belief, or personal relationships to participate. Examples should allow reasoned disagreement rather than reward conformity.

## Rights and boundaries

Submit original material you are authorized to share. Do not copy copyrighted passages, images, private curricula, or identifiable personal data without the appropriate permission. Cite sources instead of reproducing them. Do not submit Trivian Technologies implementation material or Ahava Way certification sequencing, proprietary assisted-stretch methods, assessment materials, or instructor training architecture.

A public-content license has not yet been approved. This contribution guide grants no license and requests no copyright assignment. Before accepting third-party content, maintainers must resolve the permissions needed to publish and later license it with its contributor. Public visibility is not a blanket permission for reuse. Direct reuse questions to learn@trivianinstitute.org.

## AI-assisted contribution disclosure

For substantive AI-assisted drafting, restructuring, translation, illustration, or analysis, include in the pull request: system/provider if known, approximate date, tasks assisted, material changes made by the contributor, and checks performed. Ordinary spelling correction does not require a disclosure. Never include private prompts or personal data to prove disclosure. The contributor remains responsible for accuracy, sources, accessibility, and rights. Review the resulting work rather than assuming either quality or misconduct from AI involvement.

## Review checklist

- The resource has a clear audience, purpose, and usable activity.
- Claims and examples are distinguished; competing interpretations are represented fairly.
- Links work and the material stands on its own.
- Accessibility choices are concrete and participation is voluntary.
- No personal data, restricted curriculum, or technical implementation is included.
- AI assistance and permissions are addressed.

See [community expectations](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).
