# Interaction Literacy

Status: v0.1 draft · Maturity: formative / educational

An open-commons curriculum in development at [Trivian Institute](https://trivianinstitute.org), an education-first nonprofit. It helps people participate thoughtfully in human–AI interaction: frame a task, keep authority explicit, examine evidence, and retain responsibility for judgment. It goes beyond prompt technique to the conditions of learning and decision-making.

For independent learners, educators, libraries, universities, community programs, and organizations. No coding or AI subscription is required. The first lessons suit older secondary learners and adults, with educator adaptation to local requirements.

## Begin now

Have 20 minutes? Try [the interaction map](exercises/interaction-map.md). Have an hour? Choose one complete module:

| Module | Question | Time | Takeaway |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [01 — From prompting to interaction](curriculum/01-from-prompting-to-interaction.md) | What shapes the exchange? | 60 min | An interaction map and a revised request |
| [02 — Capability, authority, and delegation](curriculum/02-capability-authority-delegation.md) | What may happen, and who decides? | 75 min | A bounded delegation brief |
| [03 — Verification and epistemic discipline](curriculum/03-verification.md) | What supports this answer? | 75 min | A claim ledger and defensible conclusion |

Read [Foundations](FOUNDATIONS.md) for the conceptual frame, or start directly with a lesson. Each contains objectives, examples, activities, reflection, facilitator guidance, and assessment.

## Across the Commons

Apply delegation reasoning to [The Manager Who Delegated Judgment](https://github.com/TrivianInstitute/relational-ai-casebook/blob/main/cases/case-004-manager/scenario.md). The casebook develops judgment through unresolved fictional situations. Optional [decision pauses](https://github.com/TrivianInstitute/embodiment-commons/blob/main/practices/03-decision-pause.md) support noticing one's condition before reasoning; they do not validate a decision.

Trivian Institute develops educational public goods. Trivian Technologies develops technical architecture, protocols, software, and deployable systems. This curriculum distributes no TRIA implementation and does not certify technical or professional competence.
