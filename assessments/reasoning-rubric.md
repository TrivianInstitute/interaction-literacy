# Formative reasoning rubric

[Home](../README.md)

This is an educational feedback aid, not a validated instrument or certification assessment. Evaluate the reasoning artifact, not the learner's identity, confidence, bodily participation, or agreement with a doctrine. Allow oral, written, or diagrammatic responses.

| Dimension | Emerging | Developing | Demonstrated in this task |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Framing | Repeats request | Names a constraint | Names purpose, affected people, and a checkable assumption |
| Authority | Equates access with permission | Identifies one boundary | Specifies scope, changed conditions, and accountable route |
| Evidence | Accepts or rejects by tone | Finds a relevant source | Explains source-to-claim fit and limits |
| Alternatives | Gives one unexamined answer | Names another option | Fairly weighs competing choices and consequences |
| Revision | Defends initial answer regardless | Changes when prompted | Names evidence or conditions that would change the conclusion |

Use only dimensions relevant to the lesson; do not create a misleading total score. Return one strength, one next question, and an opportunity to revise.

**Transfer prompt:** A museum agent recommends canceling an event because registrations are low. It can email ticket holders. The organizer previously asked it to maximize attendance, but also promised a community group the event would run. What should happen next, what evidence matters, and what may the agent do now? Ask for reasoning without the worksheet first. Compare with the learner's earlier work; do not claim causal improvement from a single comparison.
