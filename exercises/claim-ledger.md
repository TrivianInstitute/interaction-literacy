# Exercise — Check a persuasive proposal

[Exercises](README.md) · 30 minutes · Offline evidence packet

**Purpose:** distinguish what a source says from what someone concludes from it. All organizations, numbers, and documents below are invented teaching material, not research evidence.

## Proposed AI-generated summary

“Harbor Library's AI tutor improved learning by 40%, worked equally well for every learner, and should replace the staffed evening help desk. The pilot proves that learners prefer AI support.”

## Packet

**A — Attendance note:** 20 adults volunteered for a four-week pilot. Four stopped attending. No reasons were recorded. Sixteen completed a final questionnaire. There was no comparison group.

**B — Questionnaire:** 12 of the 16 respondents selected “I would like this option to continue.” Four selected “unsure.” The questionnaire did not ask respondents to choose between AI support and a human help desk.

**C — Facilitator log:** the average number of completed practice items per session increased from 5 to 7 among the 16 completers. Tasks varied between sessions. No delayed assessment or independent measure of learning was collected. A human facilitator was present throughout.

**D — Budget memo:** the pilot's subscription charge was lower than evening staffing costs. Staff preparation, troubleshooting, accessibility support, and device costs were not included in the comparison.

## Instructions

For each claim in the summary, fill in: exact claim; relevant packet item; what it supports; what it does not support; supported/contradicted/unresolved within this packet; next useful check. Then write a recommendation of no more than 120 words.

Do not search online to corroborate this fictional pilot. The purpose is to reason from a bounded packet. For a real investigation, verify the existence, provenance, and completeness of the records as well.

## Facilitator discussion key

- Five to seven is a 40% increase in completed items, not a demonstrated 40% increase in learning. Task differences and attrition complicate interpretation.
- Equal benefit for every learner is unresolved; no subgroup or individual outcome evidence is supplied.
- Replacing the help desk does not follow from a pilot conducted with human support. The packet does not establish the outcome without that support.
- Twelve respondents favor continuing an option. That does not establish a preference for AI over staffed help, nor describe all 20 volunteers.
- Lower subscription costs are not a complete cost comparison.

A justified recommendation could preserve the option for a limited follow-up while collecting comparable learning measures, costs, and voluntary feedback about access barriers. A justified alternative could defer expansion until those questions are addressed. Grade the reasoning and consequences considered, not which recommendation is chosen.

**Reflection:** name one result that would weaken your preferred recommendation.

**Access:** offer large print, read the packet aloud, or allow a verbal ledger. Arithmetic is provided so the central task remains inference.
