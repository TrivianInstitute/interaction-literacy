# Running a session

[Home](../README.md)

Before teaching, read the chosen module and its exercise. Announce the learning goal, expected time, and ways to participate. Provide the materials in advance where possible. No AI account, personal story, bodily practice, or public speaking is compulsory. Use fictional data. If live AI is optional, follow your institution's current access and privacy requirements and retain an equivalent offline route.

Begin with individual reasoning before group comparison so the first confident speaker does not define the whole discussion. Ask learners to name their assumptions. When they disagree, separate factual questions, value judgments, and permission boundaries. Invite the strongest version of a competing interpretation. Do not score agreement with the facilitator or with Trivian propositions.

Use a visible time budget and permit breaks. Offer written responses, paired conversation, or quiet observation with a private exit response. Ask what accommodation would help without requesting a diagnosis. Learners may decline personal examples and still complete every task.

For an optional embodied pause, invite rather than direct. No touch, closed eyes, breath manipulation, or interpretation of another person's state is needed. Offer a purely conceptual pause: write the decision and one missing fact. If someone becomes uncomfortable, stop the activity for them without making their experience a teaching example.

Close with one decision, one reason, and one revision condition. Collect voluntary feedback about confusion and accessibility without identifiable histories. Record what you changed between sessions. A satisfied group does not establish that the curriculum improves long-term judgment.
