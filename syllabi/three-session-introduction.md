# Three-session introduction

[Home](../README.md)

For adult or educator-adapted older secondary learners. No prerequisites beyond access to the text in an appropriate format. Complete sessions in order or select one independently.

| Session | Preparation | In-session work | Evidence of learning |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 — 60 min | Read Foundations | Module 01 and interaction map | Revised task and explanation |
| 2 — 75 min | Bring a fictional delegation example | Module 02 and delegation brief | Scope plus response to a changed condition |
| 3 — 75 min | Read module 03 example | Claim ledger and comparison | Recommendation with limitations and revision condition |

Between sessions, observe a low-stakes interaction or an institutional workflow without collecting personal data. Write one question it raises. Observation may concern a fictional example or a published description; no live AI participation is required.

Use the formative rubric for feedback, allow revision, and avoid a pass/fail credential. After the sequence, offer the museum transfer prompt. Gather accessibility and teaching feedback before expanding the curriculum. Further domains are listed in the curriculum roadmap.
